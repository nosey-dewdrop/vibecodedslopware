privacy & your code

last updated 10 july 2026

the short version: your source code is never stored on our servers. it is read inside your own browser, and it leaves your device only as small snippets, in transit, when you ask us to generate questions — never saved, never logged. our database holds numbers (your progress), not code.

who we are

vibecodedflopware is a project by nosey-dewdrop. for privacy questions, reach us through the project repository. this policy is written to meet Turkey's KVKK (Law No. 6698) and the EU GDPR.

what happens to your code

  • fetched by your browser. when you connect a public repository, your browser downloads it directly from GitHub. the code does not pass through our servers.
  • analyzed on your device. we parse your code and build its map entirely in your browser (WebAssembly). the structure of your code never leaves your machine.
  • never stored by us. your code is not written to any database, log, backup, or cache under our control. ever.
  • snippets in transit only. to generate questions, small selected snippets are sent to an AI provider through a stateless relay. they are used to produce your questions and are not retained by us. the relay keeps no request bodies.
  • questions stay on your device. generated questions are stored locally in your browser (IndexedDB), not on our servers.

what we actually store

if you create an account, we store only:

  • account basics needed to sign you in (e.g. your email or a provider id);
  • progress numbers: mastery percentages, streaks, counts of questions solved, and a one-way hashed identifier for each repository — never the repository's name, file names, or any code.

someone with full access to our database could see your high score. they could not see a single line of your code, or even which project it was.

legal basis (KVKK / GDPR)

we process the minimal account and progress data above on the basis of your consent and of our legitimate interest in providing the service you asked for. we do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not profile you.

third parties

  • GitHub — your browser talks to GitHub directly to fetch repositories; their terms and privacy policy apply to that connection.
  • AI provider — receives only the snippets needed to generate questions, in transit, under a zero-retention configuration.
  • hosting — the site is served as static files over HTTPS; standard access logs (IP, timestamp) may be kept by the host for security.

your rights

under KVKK and GDPR you can access, correct, or delete your data, and withdraw consent at any time. deleting a repository in the app erases all of its local data — questions and cached analysis — from your device. deleting your account removes your stored progress numbers. contact us through the repository to exercise any of these rights.

cookies & local storage

we use your browser's local storage for your theme choice, your questions, and cached analysis. we do not use tracking or advertising cookies.

changes

if this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. material changes will be announced in the project repository.
