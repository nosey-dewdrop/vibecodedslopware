slopware 101: what this series is and why I am writing it¶

Everyone says the word, nobody defines it.

I am opening a new writing series called slopware 101. Many people make fun of slopware in the internet. Actually, I hate needless jokes made just to fit in. But also, as far as I see, lack of technical depth gave the localhost:3000 owners a courage of a superhero. So, I decided to create a blog about "how to avoid slopware" based on my own experiences & I prepared 24 posts.

I believe everyone should benefit from tech. It is not just business and money but making products for your own needs. If you use AI well, anyone can do anything. (Where is engineering gone? Engineering makes the difference on thinking actually, on knowing what to make, on connecting technologies and developing them.)

Here is the series I want everyone to know:

Afterwards I will write about LLM system design, orchestration, loops, workflows and gates.

Every post opens with a real incident and closes with the thing you can do tomorrow. Post 8 is a scan of 1,645 apps built on one AI builder, 170 of them on databases anyone could read and write, and the fix is one line of SQL.

You can subscribe to the newsletter at the bottom of this page. There are only three posts up right now. (I don't want to stretch the schedule because 2 months in tech = 10 years of development in any topic.)

Some posts go out on LinkedIn, some go to Instagram, and a video series will be on YouTube.

I respect anyone who is dreaming and building something.
